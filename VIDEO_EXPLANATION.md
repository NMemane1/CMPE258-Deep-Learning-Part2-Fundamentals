# Video Explanation

A single recorded walkthrough that explains all six notebooks in this repo —
going through the code and the outputs for each one.

## Watch here

**https://docs.google.com/videos/d/1mfHPylhUj8DrhpyJAZH_3cu2eN_P5gFBH5c3Az0blXA/edit?usp=sharing**

## What the video covers

The video walks through each notebook in order:

1. **Classification & Regression Metrics** — confusion matrix, accuracy and its
   limits, precision/recall, F1, ROC/AUC, and regression metrics.
2. **Hyperparameter Tuning** — learning rate finder, grid search, random
   search, and Optuna.
3. **Optimizers** — gradient descent through Momentum, RMSprop, and Adam.
4. **Activation Functions** — why non-linearity matters, sigmoid/tanh, ReLU and
   its variants, and GELU.
5. **CNN Fundamentals** — convolution, pooling, and a full CIFAR-10 classifier.
6. **Modern CNN Architectures** — ResNet, EfficientNet, and transfer learning.

Each notebook also has its own `walkthrough.md` with a section-by-section
written explanation.
